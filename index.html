Carbon cycle · Paleoclimate · AI

Earth’s past.
Climate’s future.

I’m Jinlong Du, a postdoctoral researcher at the State Key Laboratory of Marine Geology, Tongji University. I study how the ocean and land shape Earth’s carbon cycle and climate.

My work spans glacial cycles and geological timescales, combining box models, Earth System Models of Intermediate Complexity, and GCM-based biogeochemistry. With colleagues, I am developing AI approaches to connect long-term carbon–climate dynamics with future change.
